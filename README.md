Forsta is the customer experience and market research technology company formed from the 2021 merger of Confirmit, FocusVision and Dapresy, and acquired by Press Ganey in 2022 to trade as PG Forsta. Its HX Platform spans survey data collection (Decipher / Forsta Surveys), panel and sample management, qualitative research, text and social analytics, and dashboard visualization for customer, employee and brand experience programs. Forsta publishes eight OpenAPI documents covering 345 operations at docs.developer.focusvision.com: the Decipher REST API for survey automation and data extraction, the Panel Management Integration, Community, webhook and vendor-callback contracts, and the Sample Marketplace partner contract that sample providers must implement to be listed.

**Areas:** Customer Insights · Feedback · Market Research · Surveys · Customer Experience · Employee Experience · Panel Management · Data Collection · Analytics · Voice of the Customer

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Forsta: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
