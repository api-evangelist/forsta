Market research agencies are under pressure to deliver faster, charge less, and provide more strategic value. Collecting reliable data is still essential, but protecting margins now means turning rigorous research into clear, decision-ready insights without adding weeks of manual analysis and reporting. That shift changes what it means to be a research agency.
